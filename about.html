OVER 10 YEARS OF GREAT ROCKWELL

The story of SAKURA Real Estate commenced in 2000 with the conversion of a disused thermal power plant into a flourishing, self-sufficient community. Rebranded as the Sakura Center, this pioneering project marked the inception of numerous outstanding communities that set the standard for residential, recreational, and commercial excellence.

As part of the Lopez Group of Companies, SAKURA Real Estate consistently elevates living standards by crafting communities that seamlessly integrate residences, workspaces, and lifestyle destinations. Each development bears the hallmark of Rockwell: innovation, exclusivity, and unmatched quality.

OUR PURPOSE

One of SAKURA's keys to success is its perceptive understanding of the individual. Since life is not restricted to the four-walled structure of a home, the property maverick recognized the need to extend its breadth. Today, SAKURA's signature of exclusivity and luxury is felt even outside the comforts of your residence.

VISION

At Sakura Real Estate, our mission is to redefine urban living by creating vibrant, sustainable communities that foster connectivity, well-being, and prosperity. We are committed to delivering innovative spaces that harmonize with the environment, exceed expectations, and enhance the quality of life for generations to come.

MISSION

Our vision at Sakura Real Estate is to be the foremost leader in revolutionizing urban landscapes, setting new benchmarks for excellence in design. Guided by our commitment to innovation and integrity, we aspire to shape environments where people thrive, leaving a lasting legacy of inspiration and progress.
